Batch Image Processing Example
One prompt, and AiPy handles watermarking, compression, renaming, and format conversion in bulk — no screen-watching required.

Scenario
A photography enthusiast with 5 years of experience faces the same pain points every time after a shoot:

RAW format conversion: Camera files are huge and must be exported to JPG one by one
Batch copyright watermarking: Adding watermarks in Photoshop one by one is extremely time-consuming
Compression & optimization: Original photos range from a few MB to tens of MB and need compression to meet platform limits
Messy asset organization: Camera auto-naming is meaningless, and manual renaming is tedious
Goal: Process 50 photos in one go — watermark, compress, rename, and convert to PNG.
Steps
Open AiPy and install the agent Graphic & Ad Design (image2) from the marketplace
Upload the images to process and enter the prompt (see "Original Prompt" below)
AiPy automatically generates and runs Python code — no screen-watching required
