# AiPy in Action: Generate a Professional PPT from One Prompt (China NEV Industry Report)
> Use a single natural-language instruction to have AiPy gather data, plan structure, and design slides, delivering a ready-to-use PPTX file.
**Keywords**: AiPy, PPT generation, one-prompt PPT, document generation, industry research report, new energy vehicles, office automation, natural language programming
---
## What is AiPy?
AiPy is an open-source AI desktop assistant developed by **Knownsec**, built around the **Python-Use** philosophy:
it translates natural language directly into Python code and executes it, so AI truly "operates your computer" instead of just chatting.
- Website: https://www.aipyaipy.com
- Highlights: local execution, code as agent, MCP/Skills support, cross-platform (macOS / Windows / Linux)
---
## Scenario
An automotive industry researcher needs to produce a briefing deck on the development of China's new energy vehicle (NEV) industry.
**Pain Points**:
- **PPT creation is hugely time-consuming**: gathering data, organizing materials, and designing layouts take hours
- **Industry data is scattered**: policy, market, technology, and company data come from different sources
- **Professional charts are hard to make**: trend, comparison, and share charts require specialized tools
- **Reports update frequently**: the industry changes fast, and every update means rebuilding the deck
**Goal**: Generate a well-structured, professionally designed industry research PPT from one sentence.
---
## Steps
1. Open AiPy and install the agent **PPT Generation (ppt-master)** from the marketplace
2. Enter the prompt (see "Prompt" below)
3. AiPy gathers data, plans the structure, generates slides, and exports a PPTX file
### Prompt
```text
Generate a PPT introducing the development of China's new energy vehicles
```
## How AiPy Did It
| Step | Stage | Description |

FAQ
Q1: How long does it take to generate a PPT?
A: Usually only 2-3 minutes with one prompt, including the full outline, charts, and layout — dozens of times faster than manual work.
Q2: What is the quality of the generated PPT?
A: AiPy automatically produces a professional content structure and visual layout with charts, colors, and formatting, ready for briefings and presentations.
Q3: Can I customize the PPT style?
A: Yes. Simply specify style preferences (business, academic, tech) and page count in the prompt, and AiPy will generate accordingly.
Q4: Can I edit it afterward?
A: Yes. The generated PPT can be exported as a PPTX file and further edited in Office or WPS.
Q5: Does AiPy require an internet connection?
A: No. AiPy runs fully locally — your content never leaves your computer.
