# AiPy in Action: Generate a Siyangzun Fridge Magnet Cultural Product Proposal from One Prompt (4 Styles + F1–F10 Constraints)
> Upload a photo taken in a museum, and AiPy verifies the artifact archive, extracts feature constraints, generates 4 style variations, and delivers a ready-to-use HTML proposal.
**Keywords**: AiPy, cultural product proposal, Siyangzun, fridge magnet, museum merchandise, AI design, Graphic & Design, web frontend development, licensing compliance, natural language programming

## What is AiPy?
AiPy is an open-source AI desktop assistant developed by **Knownsec**, built around the **Python-Use** philosophy:
it translates natural language directly into Python code and executes it, so AI truly "operates your computer" instead of just chatting.
- Website: https://www.aipyaipy.com

## Scenario
A cultural product planner needs to create a single-category fridge magnet proposal for the museum artifact "Siyangzun" (Four-Ram Square Zun).
**Pain Points**:
- **High barrier to proposals**: a cultural product proposal requires design, copywriting, and market expertise working together, making it hard for an individual to produce a complete plan alone
- **Easy to distort the artifact**: the Siyangzun has intricate details — curled-horn ram heads, flanges, and banana-leaf patterns — and any error invites criticism from experts
- **Outsourcing is expensive and slow**: hiring a design agency for a single-category proposal takes days to weeks, with high costs and repeated back-and-forth
- **Hard to reuse renderings**: AI-generated images lack enforced constraint prompts, producing wildly inconsistent artifact depictions that fail acceptance checks
**Goal**: From a single photo, generate a complete proposal with 4 style renderings, craftsmanship and pricing, and compliance statements.
---
## Steps
1. Open AiPy and install the **Graphic & Design (image2)** agent and the **Web Frontend Development** Skill from the marketplace
2. Upload a museum photo of the Siyangzun and enter the prompt (see "Prompt" below)
3. AiPy verifies the artifact archive, extracts F1–F10 feature constraints, generates 4 style renderings, and fills in craftsmanship, pricing, and compliance statements
4. Refine conversationally: say "change the style" or "adjust the pricing" and get instant revisions
### Prompt
```text
Based on the photo of the Siyangzun I took in the museum, create a single-category fridge magnet cultural product proposal: first verify the authoritative archive information of the artifact and extract a list of artifact features that must not be distorted; then call the Graphic & Design agent to design 4 different style fridge magnet concepts around the same artifact (Cute National Treasure, Cyber Bronze, Ink Wash, Minimalist Relief), providing an AI design rendering and a copyable enforced-constraint prompt for each style; embed the generated images in the corresponding positions, then add craftsmanship, pricing, market positioning, and licensing compliance notes, and turn it into a polished HTML proposal page.
```

## FAQ
**Q1: Does AiPy require an internet connection?**
A: This case needs to verify the authoritative artifact archive online, so an internet connection is required. AiPy itself runs fully locally and never uploads your data.
**Q2: Do I need design skills?**
A: No. Just describe the artifact and product category in plain language, and AiPy handles design, copywriting, and layout.
**Q3: Which operating systems does AiPy support?**
A: macOS / Windows / Linux desktops.
**Q4: Can the generated renderings be reproduced?**
A: Yes. Each rendering includes F1–F10 enforced constraint prompts that reproduce the same design.
**Q5: Can the proposal be used directly in business discussions?**
A: Yes. It includes craftsmanship, pricing, market positioning, and licensing compliance statements, though museum licensing and expert review are still required before mass production.
